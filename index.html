Live points = official points − points from last year's edition of each tournament being played + points secured at this year's edition (a player who has reached a round keeps at least that round's points even if the match is still in progress). Tournament levels come from Wikipedia's season calendars (CC BY-SA); where a tournament can't be matched, its level is guessed and flagged. Players level on live points share a rank (shown as T41) because the tours' tiebreak, tournaments played, isn't in the free data. Singles only. Qualifying points and some early-round points are approximate. Data: unofficial public ESPN feeds for ATP/WTA rankings and scores, which can lag or change without notice.
